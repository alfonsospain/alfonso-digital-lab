Alfonso Digital Lab

Open Linguistic Technologies

About

Alfonso Digital Lab is an open-access collection of digital tools developed by Alfonso Rodríguez Fernández-Peña, University of Oviedo, for research, teaching and knowledge transfer in phonetics, translation, localization, forensic linguistics and language technologies.
